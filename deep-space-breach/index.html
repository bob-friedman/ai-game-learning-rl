DEEP SPACE BREACH - TACTICAL MANUAL

I. MISSION LORE & DOCTRINE

Following a distress beacon, your squad has boarded the derelict freighter USG Ishimura-7. The vessel is infested. Due to the tight architecture, heavy vehicle support is impossible.

II. COMBAT & REFLEX FIRE

Reflex Fire System: If a unit survives an attack, they will immediately return fire (counterattack) provided the attacker is within their Range and Line of Sight.

Tactical Note: Trading shots with a Brute (HP: 22) or Heavy (HP: 16) is lethal. Only engage high-HP targets from outside their weapon range or while they are behind weak cover.
